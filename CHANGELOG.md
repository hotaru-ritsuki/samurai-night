## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- Oni color palette: ink-black surfaces, bone text, one vermilion accent.
- Four still backdrop scenes: `torii-dusk`, `red-fuji`, `ink-sea`, `blossom-ridge`.
- Automatic scene rotation every 20 minutes.
- `/scene` command to switch to the next scene or a named one.
